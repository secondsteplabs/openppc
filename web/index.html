Turn a rule from the open rulebook into something that runs on its own, without giving OpenPPC access to your account.

  1. 1Pick a rule

    Start from the rulebook, for example: add a negative once the waste model is 90% sure a search term is bad.

  2. 2Test it on your export

    See what it would have flagged, and what those search terms did next, before it touches anything.

  3. 3Install a script

    Get a Google Ads Script to paste into your own account. It previews by default and changes nothing until you say so.
