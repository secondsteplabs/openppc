# Terms that look bad but need more clicks

**What the audit found:** {n_terms} terms have no conversions yet but are not proven bad. The closest, {closest_term}, is {closest_chance} likely bad after {closest_clicks} clicks. The waste model needs about {clicks_needed} clicks without a conversion to be 90% sure.

## Why it matters
A term with no conversions after a handful of clicks may simply be unlucky. Blocking it too early can cut off searches that would have converted next week. Waiting until the evidence is strong protects good traffic.

## How to fix it in Google Ads
1. Leave these terms running for now. Don't add them as negatives yet.
2. Note them, and run the audit again when the account has had more traffic, usually next month.
3. If a term is clearly irrelevant to what you sell, for example a job search or a product you don't offer, you can add it as a negative now. That's a judgment about relevance, not about the numbers. [Shiv]

## Check before you change anything
If the budget is tight and a term is expensive per click, review it sooner. The audit lists each term's cost so you can decide.

## How to tell it worked
On the next audit, each term either converts, stays unproven, or moves to the proven-waste list with enough clicks behind it.
