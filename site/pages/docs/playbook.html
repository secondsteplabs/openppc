The OpenPPC Playbook

An audit tells you what is wrong. The Playbook tells you how to fix it: for every finding, why it matters, the steps in Google Ads, what to check first and how to tell it worked.

How it works

Run the Playbook on an export and it runs the audit, then turns each finding into a fix plan. The plan opens with the audit's own sentence for that finding, so every number in it is one the audit computed and checked. The guides add no numbers of their own and promise no results. Each step cites the rule in OpenPPC's rulebook and the source behind it: Google's help pages, named practitioners, or OpenPPC's own reasoning.

On your computer:

$ openppc playbook search-term-waste search_terms.csv --brand "Your Brand"

Try it on the made-up sample account with openppc playbook search-term-waste --sample. In Claude, Cursor and other assistants, the MCP server's fix_plan_for_account tool does the same. OpenPPC never changes your account: you make each change in Google Ads.
