Support

Get help with OpenPPC

OpenPPC is free and open source, made by Second Step Labs. Here is how to reach us and fix the most common problems.

Contact

  • Email: support@openppc.si. We usually reply within two working days.
  • Bugs and ideas: open an issue on GitHub. Please use the fictional sample files, or remove client names and figures, before attaching anything.
  • Security issues: report them privately through GitHub's private vulnerability reporting, never in a public issue.

OpenPPC in ChatGPT

It asks me for an export

OpenPPC works from Google Ads exports, never from a live account. In Google Ads, open Campaigns, then Insights and reports, then Search terms (or Keywords), pick a date range and download the report as CSV. Upload it in the chat and ask for an audit.

It says the export is too large

Uploads are limited to 25 MB. Choose a shorter date range, or run OpenPPC on your computer, which handles any size.

It says I reached the fair-use limit

The hosted server allows 30 audits or checks per 10 minutes for each user. Wait a few minutes, or use the browser app or the command line, which have no limits.

It won't change my account or connect to it

That's by design. OpenPPC is read-only: it tells you what to change, and you make the change in Google Ads.
