# Responsive search ads: too few assets, too few ads, too much pinning

**What the audit found:** {found}

## Why it matters
A responsive search ad mixes its headlines and descriptions to fit each search. Fewer assets, fewer ads and heavy pinning all leave Google fewer combinations to test.

## How to fix it in Google Ads
1. Fill each ad toward Google's maximum of 15 headlines and 4 descriptions. Each headline should say something different: the service, the location, a benefit, an offer, a reason to trust you.
2. Run 2 or 3 responsive search ads in each ad group, each rated Good or Excellent for Ad Strength.
3. Pin only when you must, for example a required legal line. If you pin, pin 2 or 3 different assets to the same position rather than one.

## Check before you change anything
Ad Strength is a writing aid. Google doesn't use it in the auction, so judge ads by their conversions, not their rating.

## How to tell it worked
The next audit shows more ads with enough assets, every ad group with 2 or more ads, and no fully pinned ads.
