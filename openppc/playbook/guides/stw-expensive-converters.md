# Terms that convert, but at too high a cost

**What the audit found:** {found}

## Why it matters
These terms bring results, so blocking them is usually wrong. But each conversion costs far more than your average, and that drags the whole account's cost per conversion up.

## How to fix it in Google Ads
1. First check what those conversions were worth. If they are good leads or sales, an expensive conversion can still be profitable.
2. If they are poor leads, add the term as an exact match negative, as for proven waste.
3. If they are good leads at a cost you can't afford, move the term to its own ad group with a tighter ad and landing page, or, on manual bidding, lower its bid. On Smart Bidding, set a cost per conversion target and let bidding adjust.

## Check before you change anything
A term with only one conversion can look expensive by chance. Give it more time before you act unless its cost is clearly out of line.

## How to tell it worked
Next month, the term's cost per conversion should be closer to your account's average, or the term should be gone.
