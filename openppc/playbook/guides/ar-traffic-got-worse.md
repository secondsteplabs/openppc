# More spend bought worse traffic

**What the audit found:** {found}

## Why it matters
When spend or reach grows but conversions don't keep up, the extra money is buying less relevant searches. It is the most expensive pattern to leave running.

## How to fix it in Google Ads
1. Open Change history for the period and find what changed: a budget increase, new broad match keywords, a new campaign, or a bidding change.
2. Run the search-term audit for the same period, and add negatives for the irrelevant searches the change brought in.
3. If one change clearly caused it, undo it, or scale it back halfway and watch for two weeks.

## Check before you change anything
Make sure tracking is sound and the period isn't a seasonal dip. See the guides on tracking and on holding steady.

## How to tell it worked
On the next period's account read, cost per conversion should fall back toward the earlier period's.
