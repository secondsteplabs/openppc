# Where your budget goes by match type

**What the audit found:** {found}

## Why it matters
Broad match reaches the most searches, and with it the most irrelevant ones. When broad match takes a growing share of the budget at a higher cost per conversion, money is drifting to the least controlled traffic.

## How to fix it in Google Ads
1. If broad match costs more per conversion than exact, check that its campaigns use Smart Bidding. Broad match without Smart Bidding is its own finding.
2. Review broad match's search terms every week and add negatives for anything off-target.
3. If broad match still costs clearly more after that, move the converting searches it found into exact or phrase match keywords and lower broad match's share.

## Check before you change anything
Broad match often finds new converting searches. A higher cost per conversion can be worth it if those searches become exact keywords later.

## How to tell it worked
Over the next months, broad match's cost per conversion should move toward exact match's, or its share of spend should fall.
