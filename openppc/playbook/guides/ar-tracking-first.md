# Check conversion tracking first

**What the audit found:** {found}

## Why it matters
Every judgment about cost per conversion depends on conversions being counted correctly. Zero conversions usually means tracking broke, not that ads stopped working. A very high conversion rate usually means page views, clicks or other small actions are counted as conversions.

## How to fix it in Google Ads
1. Open Goals, then Conversions, and check each conversion action's status. Any that say inactive or no recent conversions need attention.
2. Submit a test lead or booking on your own site, then check it appears in Google Ads within a day.
3. Set only real leads or sales as primary conversions. Make page views, clicks and other small actions secondary, so they're recorded but not used for bidding.

## Check before you change anything
Don't change bids, budgets or keywords on these numbers until tracking is confirmed.

## How to tell it worked
After the fix, run the account read again on the next period. Conversions should be above zero and the conversion rate in a believable range for leads or sales.
