# Missing sitelinks, callouts and snippets

**What the audit found:** {found}

## Why it matters
These assets make an ad bigger and give searchers more reasons and more links to click. They cost nothing extra to add.

## How to fix it in Google Ads
1. Add at least 4 sitelinks to each campaign, each pointing to a different useful page, with a short description. Busy campaigns should have at least 6.
2. Add at least 4 callouts: short facts such as opening hours, guarantees or free quotes.
3. Add at least one structured snippet, for example a list of your services or locations.
4. Assets can be added at account, campaign or ad group level. Account-level ones cover every campaign without their own.

## Check before you change anything
Each sitelink should go to a real, distinct page. Several sitelinks pointing to the home page don't help.
