# Converting terms worth adding as keywords

**What the audit found:** {found}

## Why it matters
These searches already work. Making them keywords gives you control over their bid, their ad and their landing page, and stops other keywords competing for them.

## How to fix it in Google Ads
1. In the Search terms report, tick these terms and choose Add as keyword.
2. Add each to the ad group whose ad matched it, as an exact or phrase match keyword.
3. If a term doesn't fit any ad group's theme, start a new ad group for it with an ad written around it.

## Check before you change anything
Two conversions is a start, not proof. Check the leads or sales behind them were real before you invest more in the term.

## How to tell it worked
On the next audit these terms show as keywords, not search terms, with a similar or better cost per conversion.
