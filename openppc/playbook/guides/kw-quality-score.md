# Spend on low Quality Score keywords

**What the audit found:** {found}

## Why it matters
Quality Score is Google's diagnostic for how well a keyword, its ad and its landing page fit the searches. A low score usually means paying more for the same position. Google treats it as a diagnostic, not a target, so use it to find what to fix, not as a number to chase.

## How to fix it in Google Ads
Fix the part rated below average:
1. **Expected click-through rate:** the ad doesn't win clicks for this search. Put the keyword's words in the headline and make the offer clearer.
2. **Ad relevance:** the ad doesn't match the keyword. Move the keyword to an ad group whose ads are about the same thing, or write an ad for it.
3. **Landing page experience:** the page doesn't deliver what the ad promised. Send the click to the page about that exact service, and make it fast on mobile.

## Check before you change anything
Quality Score shows a dash when there is too little data. Ignore scores on keywords with very few impressions.

## How to tell it worked
Scores take a few weeks to update. On a later audit the parts you fixed should no longer be rated below average.
