# Hold steady: not enough data, or it's working

**What the audit found:** {found}

## Why it matters
Not every change needs a response. With too few conversions, movements are noise. In a seasonal business, comparing with last month misleads. And when the account read shows an efficiency win, steady growth or tighter targeting, the right move is to keep what's working.

## What to do
1. Too few conversions (under 10 in both periods): don't act on cost per conversion moves yet. Wait for more data.
2. Under 30 conversions a period: too few to judge Smart Bidding on. Give it longer before changing strategy or targets.
3. Seasonal business: compare with the same period last year before crediting or blaming the account.
4. A positive pattern: don't change the campaigns behind it. Note what changed, so you can repeat it.

## How to tell it worked
The next account read, with more data or a year-on-year comparison, gives a clear pattern to act on, or confirms the gain held.
