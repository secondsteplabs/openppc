# Duplicate keywords

**What the audit found:** {found}

## Why it matters
Duplicates compete with each other for the same searches. Google picks one, often not the one with the best ad or landing page, and the split history makes both harder to judge.

## How to fix it in Google Ads
1. For each duplicate, choose the ad group that fits it best: the one whose ad and landing page match the keyword.
2. Pause the copies in the other ad groups.
3. Do it by hand. Don't accept Google's automatic "remove redundant keywords" recommendation, which can pick the wrong copy.

## Check before you change anything
If the copies sit in different campaigns on purpose, for example one per location, they aren't true duplicates. Leave those.

## How to tell it worked
The next account structure audit shows no duplicates.
