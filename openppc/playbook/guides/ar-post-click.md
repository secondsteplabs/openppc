# Clicks held up, conversions didn't

**What the audit found:** {found}

## Why it matters
If the same kind of traffic arrives but fewer people convert, the problem is after the click: the landing page, the form, the offer, or tracking. Changing keywords or bids won't fix it.

## How to fix it in Google Ads and on your site
1. Submit your own form or booking on the landing page, on a phone and a computer, and check it works and arrives.
2. Check whether the landing page, prices or offer changed in the period.
3. Check conversion tracking, as in the tracking guide.
4. Check the page loads quickly on mobile.

## Check before you change anything
A drop on few conversions can be chance. If both periods have under 10 conversions, wait for more data first.

## How to tell it worked
After the fix, the next period's conversion rate should return toward the earlier period's.
