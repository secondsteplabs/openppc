# Brand and non-brand in the same campaign

**What the audit found:** {found}

## Why it matters
Brand searches convert far more cheaply than other searches. Mixed together, they hide how the non-brand keywords really perform and pull budget toward whichever is easier to win.

## How to fix it in Google Ads
1. Create a separate brand campaign with its own budget.
2. Move the brand keywords into it, and pause them in the original campaign.
3. Add your brand name as a negative in the non-brand campaign, so brand searches go only to the brand campaign.

## Check before you change anything
Moving keywords to a new campaign starts their history over. Do it on a quiet day and watch both campaigns for the first week.

## How to tell it worked
The next audit shows no campaign mixing brand and non-brand, and you can see each one's cost per conversion on its own.
