# Google Ads audit: Acme Plumbing, July 2026

**Verdict:** A healthy account with a broad-match leak.

## What we found

1. You wasted $4,200 last month on search terms that never converted.
2. "free plumbing estimate" alone burned $312 on 41 clicks with zero conversions.
3. "plumber jobs hiring" drove 22 conversions, almost all from job seekers.
4. Your cost per conversion is $58.51, healthy for home services.
5. Conversion rate fell from 9% to 14% after the July match-type changes.
6. Broad match terms account for 62% of your wasted spend.
7. Cutting the six worst terms saves about $9,000 a year.
8. Your CTR of 7.4% beats the 6.47% home-services average.

## Actions

1. Add the zero-conversion terms as negative keywords.
2. Move "same day water heater install" into its own exact-match ad group.
